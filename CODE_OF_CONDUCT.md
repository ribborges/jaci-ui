# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Jaci UI community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints,
giving and accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Harassment, insulting comments, personal attacks, publishing private information, and other
conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to contato@richardborges.dev. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or otherwise act on contributions that do
not align with this code of conduct.
